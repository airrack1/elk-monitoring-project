03 Anatomy of a box
PHASE 00

Deploy

One tap spins the box up in your browser. No VPN config, no OpenVPN pack, no waiting on a queue. You get an IP and a shell.

PHASE 01

Enumerate

Scan it. nmap the host, read the open ports, poke the services. The terminal answers like the real thing.

PHASE 02

Exploit

Find the misconfig — an exposed share, a weak credential, a SUID binary — and turn it into a foothold. Guided, never hand-held.

PHASE 03

Root & report

Escalate, grab the root flag, and the box is yours. XP banks to your profile and the next rung unlocks.
